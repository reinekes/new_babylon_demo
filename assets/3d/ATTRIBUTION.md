# 3D assets and licenses

Babylon.js 9.31.0: official npm `babylonjs@9.31.0`, bundled locally in assets/vendor, Apache-2.0 license beside the bundle. No runtime CDN or remote asset dependency.

Photographic materials downloaded20 October2026 from the official Poly Haven public asset API, 1k JPEG color and OpenGL normal maps, unmodified. Source checksums checked during acquisition.

- https://polyhaven.com/a/clay_plaster
- https://polyhaven.com/a/dry_ground_01
- https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table_worn

All three are CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license . These are material studies, not photographs of archaeological Sumerian surfaces.

Buildings, articulated procedural figures, animals, terrain and repeating brick/reed/linen maps are authored for this game in district3d/*.js. They are not Banished assets and are not hand-rigged Blender/glTF models. Archaeological forms are stylized; reference Penn Museum Mesopotamian City Life and Adaptation and Change. This first slice does not claim visual parity with a finished commercial game.

Additional riverbank ground: https://polyhaven.com/a/sparse_grass , 1k JPEG color/normal, CC0, downloaded20 October2026, checksum verified.
